# charity: water – Water Drop Game

Catch falling clean-water drops, avoid pollutants, and try to score 20 points in 30 seconds. The game uses plain HTML, CSS, and DOM-based JavaScript.

## How to play

- Select **Start Game** to begin a 30-second round.
- Click or tap blue drops to earn 1 point.
- Avoid purple pollutant drops; clicking one costs a life.
- A clean-water drop that reaches the ground also costs a life.
- The game ends when time runs out or all 3 lives are gone.
- Score 20 or more points to win. Choose **Play Again** to start a new round.

The layout adapts to smaller screens, and drops can also be activated with a keyboard.
