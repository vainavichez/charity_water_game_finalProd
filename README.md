# charity: water – Water Drop Game

Catch falling clean-water drops, avoid pollutants, and try to reach the selected score target in 30 seconds. Choose Easy, Normal, or Hard to change both the points needed to win and how quickly drops fall. The game uses plain HTML, CSS, and DOM-based JavaScript.

## How to play

- Choose a difficulty, then select **Start Game** to begin a 30-second round. The difficulty cannot be changed during a round.
- **Easy:** score 10 points; drops fall more slowly.
- **Normal:** score 20 points; drops use the standard speed.
- **Hard:** score 30 points; drops fall more quickly.
- Click or tap blue drops to earn 1 point.
- Avoid purple pollutant drops; clicking one costs a life.
- A clean-water drop that reaches the ground also costs a life.
- The game ends when time runs out or all 3 lives are gone.
- Reach the selected difficulty's target score to win. Choose **Play Again** to start a new round.

The layout adapts to smaller screens, and drops can also be activated with a keyboard.
